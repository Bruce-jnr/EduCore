# Independent teacher lesson pricing

## Reference and product decision
On 28 October 2026 REA inspected https://sirbus.org/pricing/ and clicked the Lesson Plan tab. Evidence ev_a898e75337322f590e83c27416d8b97170b45469fbd2b711615c2efd482399da records KG 5 cedis/class, Primary/JHS 10 cedis/class, SHS 30 cedis/semester, and Custom Scheme 20 cedis/class. Only public prices and descriptions were observed; authenticated checkout, entitlement scope, provider integration and private content-generation behavior were not observed.

The user explicitly chose ONE BOOKLET ONLY. EduPalGh therefore charges GH₵5 per KG booklet, GH₵10 per Primary/JHS booklet, and GH₵30 per SHS semester booklet, with later edits/re-downloads included. This is an intentional adaptation of the reference's class/semester labels, not a claim about Sirbus's private entitlement logic. Shared prices: shared/lesson-pricing.js. Public page: /#/lesson-pricing, styled using native EduPalGh theme tokens and dark mode. School subscriptions and school-scoped exports retain their separate rules.

Custom Scheme's GH₵20 reference price is visible as COMING SOON and cannot be purchased. The project does not implement scheme-document upload and automatically tailored generation. Existing notes remain teacher-entered templates and original starters; do not market them as a complete preloaded or officially approved curriculum library.

## Payment workflow
Teachers may register without a school and create/edit/save private lessons before paying. Opening the standard printable booklet/JSON export requires a PAID TeacherLessonPurchase for that exact planId and teacherId. Each new booklet has its own purchase, even in the same class/term; updating the paid booklet's title or lesson content does not change its identity or charge again. Booklet class/subject/year/period remain immutable through the update API.

The editor fetches a server-derived quote and offers Request payment review. POST /api/teacher/billing/purchases accepts ONLY planId and request UUID; neither caller amounts, products, ownership nor payment status are accepted. A single unique purchase per planId and unique UUID make retries safe. The account row is locked before creation so concurrent teacher requests serialize.

Platform → Teacher payments lists pending or paid requests (latest 100). Existing platform.schools.view/update permissions control listing/verification. The operator must independently confirm the exact amount and receipt reference, then verify the purchase. Payment status, operator ID/time, platform audit and teacher activity commit together; audit failure rolls back. Unique method/reference prevents reuse across teacher purchases. Matching verify retries are idempotent; changed receipt data for an already-paid purchase is rejected. These references are scoped to the teacher-purchase ledger, separate from the school subscription ledger.

There is NO gateway or automatic card/Mobile Money debit. The application does not independently validate a provider receipt. Configure a genuine public payment-support email/phone in Platform Settings and arrange collection outside the app before launch. The teacher quote displays those configured support contacts; it never invents a pay-to number. The operator's approval represents manual verification. No refund/revocation workflow is implemented in this change.

Server enforcement gates the product's formatted booklet preview/export. Raw lesson fields remain available to the owner for editing, and browser printing/manual copying of those fields cannot be prevented. This is not DRM and does not claim to prevent all manual reproduction. Public original sample lessons remain printable. Existing personal draft content is preserved; those personal booklets now require payment for the formatted export. School Professional/Enterprise lesson exports are unaffected.

New SHS personal booklets use Semester1/2 and default to 16 weeks; KG/Basic use term options. Legacy saved period labels are retained. The shared editor still allows up to 16 weeks, and teaches no official curriculum schedule merely from the period label.
