## Verified prices and capacities
Freemium: GH₵0, 50 students, 5 staff. Starter: GH₵400/month or GH₵4,000/year, 500 students, 30 staff. Professional: GH₵750/month or GH₵7,500/year, 1,000 students, 100 staff. Enterprise: agreed price and capacities. Annual prices are ten monthly payments, saving GH₵800 and GH₵1,500 respectively. Paid trial: one-time 14 days beginning on platform approval. These values are centralized in shared/pricing.js.

## Enforced workflow
Public /#/pricing links from landing navigation/footer and the lesson library. Plan/billing selection carries into school signup. Google school signup creates Freemium ACTIVE with no expiry and stores a requested paid plan; selecting a price never grants paid access. Existing platform school provisioning is itself approval and starts a Professional trial for exactly 14 days. A platform administrator can approve a requested paid plan, activate Freemium, update access status, configure an Enterprise quote or record a verified full subscription payment.

SubscriptionPayment is separate from student fee payments. Its amount/currency and calendar billing interval are checked server-side. Only platform.schools.update operators may verify payments. No gateway is connected and no money is charged by the app. Verification means the operator has independently checked receipt; this does not claim provider verification. Monthly/yearly access is activated only with a payment ledger entry and audit in the same transaction. Early renewal extends from the existing end, otherwise from payment verification time; buying during a trial starts the paid interval immediately. Calendar arithmetic clamps month/year ends. No automatic prorated plan changes: different plan/cycle requires current paid period to end. There are no refunds, recurring debits or gateway webhooks in this change.

Request UUID idempotency returns a prior payment without extending access twice; mismatched reuse is rejected. Unique payment-method/reference rejects duplicate processing under different UUIDs. The Tenant row is locked first for every payment, trial/plan change and student/staff insertion. Capacity counts all non-deleted records, including inactive/archived statuses, so status changes cannot free slots. The count/check and insert share one transaction; simultaneous requests serialize on the same Tenant lock. Downgrades use the same lock and reject usage above target capacities. Direct database writes or future insertion/restoration routes must adopt this protocol. This application protocol assumes transactional InnoDB tables and needs a real two-connection race test before production.

Freemium covers core records. Finance and report-card endpoints require Starter or higher. Master-sheet endpoints and school lesson-plan endpoints require Professional or higher. School users with the Teacher role need Professional; office administrators retain basic records on Freemium. Existing role permissions remain additionally required. Request-time policy and capacity checks use database state, not JWT plan claims. Independent teacher APIs/sessions are untouched by school plan enforcement. Payroll, family portals, multi-campus and AI are not advertised as available here because they are not implemented.

Existing plan names, active/trial statuses and expiry dates are preserved; existing paid and trial accounts are marked as having used a trial. Legacy TRIAL plan entitlements map to Professional until their original expiry. Legacy Enterprise with unspecified limits retains existing open capacity until an agreed quote is configured; every new Enterprise trial/payment requires explicit positive capacities and price. Default trial setting is fixed at 14 days. Existing paid/legacy accounts without access-end dates now fail closed and require administrator review. PAST_DUE now blocks school access. These compatibility changes must be reviewed before rollout.

Apply reviewed migrations, regenerate Prisma Client and restart API before using the new fields. Do not reset the database or rerun seed. Existing data over a new limit is retained, but growth and downgrades are blocked. Read-only capacity and null-period checks should precede deployment. Windows may lock Prisma's DLL while the API runs; client generation then requires stopping/restarting the user's API. Source tests and schema validation do not prove live migration, locking or provider behavior.

## Existing schools and activation checks
School administrators with settings.update can submit a review request from Pricing through POST /api/subscriptions/request. The request stores the desired plan and audit metadata, never changes the active entitlement. An active paid period keeps its billing cycle; different-cycle requests wait until its end. Platform administrators review requests in school cards. Paid access can be restored from an existing valid payment-ledger period without recording a second payment or extending its expiry.
