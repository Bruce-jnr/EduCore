# Independent teacher lesson workspace

Teacher sign-in/signup: /#/teacher/login and /#/teacher/signup. Workspace: /#/teacher/lesson-plans. Any teacher may self-register without school affiliation, a staff profile, an invitation or a school subscription. Email/password and Google accounts are supported. The public lesson library's create actions now enter this workspace. School sign-in and school-scoped booklets remain available separately.

## Separation and security
TeacherAccount, PersonalLessonPlan and TeacherActivity are separate from Tenant, User and school LessonPlan. There is no dummy school, tenant grant or employment verification. Every personal plan read/update is scoped to authenticated teacherId; personal accounts cannot access a school-wide library. Personal booklets have teaching level, subject, term and academic-year labels, not school academic foreign keys. Level/subject options are generic planning choices, not an assertion that every combination is approved curriculum.

Teacher JWTs use a context-derived signing key plus strict teacher audience/issuer/algorithm checks. School middleware cannot verify them. School JWTs cannot enter teacher endpoints. Separate browser token/user keys and unauthorized events allow both sessions to coexist. The shared UI accepts an explicit personal transport; it never sends school tokens to the teacher API. Google challenges are bound to teacher-signin/teacher-signup intents. Email matches never automatically link Google/password or school/personal accounts. Unique teacher email and Google-subject indexes reject duplicate registration. Disabled teacher accounts are rejected on every authenticated request.

Signup and personal plan writes include TeacherActivity records in the same transaction; audit failure rolls back. Revision-conditional updates reject conflicting saves. Public auth is origin/JSON checked and rate limited. Passwords require at least 12 characters and at most 72 UTF-8 bytes, stored as bcrypt hashes. Email/password signup does not claim verified email: this only grants a private personal workspace. Email verification, password reset, account linking and institutional identity verification are not implemented; Google-only accounts use Google to return. No passwords or Google credentials are stored in activity records.

## Deployment
Review and apply migration 20261028160000_independent_teacher_workspace along with earlier pending migrations, then regenerate Prisma Client and restart the API. It only creates three new tables; it does not move, change or delete school accounts/plans. Existing personal-looking school records are not automatically reassigned based on email. Rollback must preserve/export new personal data before removing tables. These migrations were prepared, not executed. Windows previously locked the Prisma engine DLL; stop your own API before regeneration if needed. Do not activate until migrations and generated client are ready.

Email/password works without Google configuration. Google requires the existing GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID and authorized frontend origin; teacher registration does not create a trial school and does not depend on PlatformSettings.registrationEnabled. CLIENT_URL must match the frontend origin for auth POSTs. Production needs HTTPS and shared/edge rate limits across API instances.
