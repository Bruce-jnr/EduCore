# Ready-to-use teacher lessons

The personal teacher workspace offers 98 versioned packs, each with twelve populated, editable weeks (1,176 lesson instances total). This includes 87 non-Mathematics class/subject packs:

| Classes | Subject and scope | Export per saved booklet |
| --- | --- | --- |

| KG 2 | Integrated learning: communication, number and community | GH₵5 |
| Primary 1–6 | One Mathematics foundations pack per class | GH₵10 |
| JHS 1 | Mathematics Number unit (original version retained) | GH₵10 |
| JHS 2 | Mathematics: number, algebra and measurement | GH₵10 |
| JHS 3 | Mathematics: algebra and applications | GH₵10 |

Coverage is a suggested 12-week unit for each class/subject currently listed in the public library, not all terms or a complete yearly course. The original eleven packs above remain unchanged. The additional Primary and JHS subjects are listed below. Teachers select the pack explicitly, preview its outline and sample, then choose their term and adapt the sequence. Packs are not assigned to an official term. Activities, examples and teacher checks are original EduPalGh material; no Sirbus collection is imported and no AI provider is used.

## Storage and payment

The options endpoint returns pack descriptions, class, subject, price, outline and sample. Creation accepts a pack ID, checks class/subject/week count, copies server-owned weeks and stores the version in existing templateVersion. Existing saved booklets are not rewritten. Saved JSON is a snapshot; edits retain ownership/revision protections. No migration is needed. Keep published IDs/versions immutable and add a new version for content revisions.

Creation and editing remain free. Existing per-booklet Paystack verification and server export gates remain unchanged. The formatted cover, scheme, weekly notes and PDF/JSON export unlock for one saved booklet after verified payment. Reordering weeks renumbers the same copy and requires saving; it does not create another purchase. Content visible in the editor is not protected by DRM.

Before expanding into additional subjects or claiming official term coverage, obtain teacher review, verify each exact curriculum reference, check worked answers and publish explicit level/subject/coverage metadata.


## Non-Mathematics subjects

- Primary 1–6: English Language, Science, Ghanaian Language, History, Our World and Our People, Creative Arts, Religious and Moral Education, Physical Education.
- Primary 4–6 additionally: Computing and French Language.
- JHS 1–3: English Language, Science, Ghanaian Language, French Language, Arabic, Creative Arts and Design, Religious and Moral Education, Physical Education and Health, Computing, Social Studies, Career Technology.

There are 24 lower-primary, 30 upper-primary and 33 JHS non-Mathematics packs. Related classes share original unit themes, with distinct supported-demonstration, comparison and independent-application targets or age-band equivalents. English also has separate lower-primary, upper-primary and JHS text sequences. The 1,176 total counts class-specific lesson instances; it does not mean 1,176 independently authored topics. These are teaching/enrichment units, not independently mapped official grade-by-grade term schemes.

Each lesson has an outcome, model/practice activity, individual task and teacher check. RME uses respectful learning about beliefs without required observance; PE provides accessible non-contact alternatives; Science avoids unsafe experiments; Computing is paper-first with no installations, account creation, real file mutations or external messages; Career Technology uses safe paper prototypes. All practical activities require school supervision and adaptation.

Ghanaian Language plans are language-neutral: teachers must choose the language and supply or confirm locally verified passages, spelling and pronunciation. French and Arabic are explicitly beginner communication units, with checked-model practice and teacher verification required for pronunciation/additional language. Arabic editor fields use automatic text direction. Do not market these as complete language collections or verified translations. History examples are fictional unless expressly identified; real facts and source material require a checked classroom reference.

The pack chooser filters by class and subject and places non-Mathematics packs before Mathematics within each class. Payment rules are unchanged: KG GH₵5, Primary/JHS GH₵10 per saved booklet. Nothing in this expansion grants export access, rewrites existing saved booklets or alters previous published pack IDs.

Practice targets are subject-specific: communication and verified models for languages; safe inclusive participation for PE; intentional exploration and revision for arts; and trace/test/improve cycles for Computing and Career Technology.
